02 — Premise

Sonder

The quiet resonance you feel with strangers whose lives you'll never know. Turning outward loosens the grip of your own story: less alone, less quick to judge, more aware that everyone around you is carrying a life as full and unfinished as your own.

03 — Origin

Born from the quiet perspective that comes with travelling alone.

In unfamiliar places, the world feels larger than any single story. Strangers passing by stop being background figures. They become people moving through lives just as full, complex, and meaningful as any other.

A fleeting glance, a conversation overheard, a stranger disappearing into a crowd. Each offers only a fragment of a life that continues long after the moment passes.

A reminder that life doesn't have to move at the pace of a race, and there is value in slowing down, paying attention, and seeing beyond a single perspective.

Sonder is a fragrance born from that feeling: a reminder of the lives unfolding all around us, most of which will remain unknown.
