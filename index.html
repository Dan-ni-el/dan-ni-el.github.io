★ Featured · BEng Dissertation, IE4100R

Measuring Supply-Chain Disruption on Shipping Lead Times

Solo · AIS data analysis of Singapore ports · AY 2025/26

Using 2024 AIS vessel-tracking data, I measured how the Red Sea Crisis drove congestion and vessel delays across Singapore's ports. Built a multi-metric congestion framework (berth utilisation, anchorage and turnaround time, arrival frequency, congested-vessel counts), evaluated Keppel Terminal's emergency reactivation, quantified spillover to Port Klang and Port of Tanjung Pelapas, then ran a service-failure analysis against pre-disruption baselines.

The finding: March to May congestion was demand-driven, not operational failure. Keppel's reactivation gave meaningful but partial relief, and no large-scale permanent diversion materialised, leaving an asymmetric resilience gap where Malaysian ports (especially Port of Tanjung Pelapas) stayed more exposed. Closed with four policy recommendations, including a data-calibrated 10-15% strategic buffer for Tuas Mega Port.
